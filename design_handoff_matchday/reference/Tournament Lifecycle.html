What changed from v1

  • New step 3 — Seed & schedule. Generating the draw no longer also publishes it. The organizer first closes registration, then reviews/reorders seeds (drag), then sets each fixture’s time & venue. “Go live” is the last tap — so the bracket is never public before it’s ready.
  • Precise status words. A team moves Requested → Accepted → In. “Accepted” means approved but unpaid; “In” = approved and paid (= counts toward the draw). No more contradictory “Confirmed · Unpaid”.
  • Track-only money. matchday never processes payments — it tracks cash you collect. “Mark paid” just records that money changed hands. Stated on the desk so there’s no false expectation.
  • Closing & failure paths. Registration closes automatically at the cap or by tapping Close registration. If fewer than the minimum joined by the deadline, the desk shows a “not enough teams” banner with Extend / Lower size / Cancel — not a dead end.
  • Byes for odd counts. 5–7 teams in a knockout auto-assign byes to top seeds; the seed list shows them, so non-power-of-2 cups just work.
  • Phase 4 hub is unchanged from what you built — Bracket/Table · Fixtures · Teams · Manage — it simply starts populated because seeding & scheduling already happened.
