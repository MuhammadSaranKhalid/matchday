Delete your Matchday account

Delete in the app

Open Matchday → menu → Settings → Delete account. Read the consequences, type DELETE and confirm. Keep the app open while deletion completes. If a network error interrupts deletion, retry or contact support; some uploaded files may already have been removed.

Request deletion without the app

Email muhammadsarankhalid@gmail.com from the address used for your Matchday account with the subject “Delete my Matchday account”. Include your username if you know it. If your Google sign-in uses a different address, tell us which account you want removed. We will verify ownership before deletion. Never send a password or one-time sign-in code. The email link below opens your email app; send the message there to submit your request.

What is deleted

Your sign-in account and personal profile, authored posts and comments, follows, notification tokens and uploaded files are removed. Messages you sent are replaced with deletion notices. Shared cricket records may remain with your account identity removed, labeled Deleted player. Shared teams can transfer to a remaining manager or be archived.

What can remain

People who received your content may have their own copies. Provider backups and security logs follow the providers’ retention practices and may not disappear immediately. Ask support about a particular retained item. Account deletion cannot be undone.
