Privacy policy

Who operates Matchday

Matchday is operated by Muhammad Saran Khalid. For privacy questions, contact muhammadsarankhalid@gmail.com. Effective 14 September 2026.

Information we use

We process your sign-in email and account ID, profile name, username and photos; optional profile and cricket details; team memberships, invitations, fixtures, scores and tournament records; posts, comments, messages and images you submit; follows, blocks, reports and notification preferences. If you choose location features, we use the location you enter or permit your device to provide. We use device push tokens to deliver notifications. Our hosting and authentication providers may process network information such as IP addresses and request logs for operation and security.

Why we use it

We use this information to provide accounts, organize cricket, show scorecards, deliver messages and notifications, respond to support requests, and investigate abuse. Matchday does not include advertising or sell personal data. Optional photos, location access and notifications can be declined; some related features will then be unavailable.

Who can see it

Profiles, public posts, teams and match records can be visible to other users. Messages are presented to chat participants. Uploaded images are served using hosted URLs; anyone who receives an image URL may be able to open or retain a copy. Do not upload sensitive documents or confidential images. Other users may keep screenshots or copies of content you share.

Service providers and transfers

We use Supabase for authentication, databases, file storage and backend services; Google sign-in and Firebase Cloud Messaging for authentication and push notifications; and location/search providers when those features are used. Data may be processed outside Pakistan. The support website is hosted through OpenAI Sites and its infrastructure providers. Network connections use HTTPS. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted.

Retention and deletion

Account information is kept while your account remains active. Settings → Delete account removes your account, personal profile, authored posts and comments, follows, push tokens and uploaded files. Messages you sent are replaced with deletion notices. Shared cricket scorecards can remain with your account identity removed and the label Deleted player. Team ownership may transfer to another manager, or the team may be archived. Copies held by other users and provider backup/security retention are not instantly erased by deletion. Contact us for a specific data-removal request or questions about provider retention.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, manage notifications, report content, block users and delete your account in Settings. You can also request deletion without installing the app through the Delete account page on this website. We verify ownership before acting on an email request. Do not send passwords, one-time codes or identity documents in your request.

Adults and changes

Matchday’s initial release is intended for adults aged 18 or older. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us for review and removal. We update this page when our practices change and show the effective date above.
